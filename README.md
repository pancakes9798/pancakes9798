# Hi, I'm Emanuele 👋

Quantitative modeller in **ALM & Treasury at BNL (BNP Paribas Group)** and **PhD candidate in Quantitative Finance at Sapienza University of Rome**.
I work where econometrics meets balance-sheet risk: interest-rate and behavioural models for banks, and Bayesian methods for yield curves, climate and energy risk.

---

### 🔬 Research interests

- **Bayesian econometrics** — regime-switching and latent-state models, identification
- **Term structure modelling** — Dynamic Nelson–Siegel with time-varying transition probabilities
- **Climate & energy finance** — transition risk, power–gas markets, stress testing
- **Generative scenarios & decision making** — diffusion models, Bayesian optimisation, Gaussian processes

### 🏦 Day job

Quantitative models for ALM & Treasury: prepayment (CPR), non-maturity deposits (NMD) and client-rate models.
